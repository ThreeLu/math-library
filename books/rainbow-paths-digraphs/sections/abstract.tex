We organize the fixed-positive-density argument around one robust partial-factorization absorption theorem, one exact local matching interface, and one phase-compatible quotient assembly. Earlier complete-factorization, rectangular, primitive and periodic special cases are incorporated into these common proofs. The directed statement implies the corresponding undirected result by replacing every edge with two opposite arcs of the same colour. The counting estimates, physical-resource restrictions, quantitative matching loss and exact assembly identities are recorded explicitly; the final appendix gives their dependency order and parameter margins.
