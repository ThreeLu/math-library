\section{Dependency order and quantitative margins}\label{app:audit}
The argument has the following acyclic order. Theorem~\ref{rpe:theorem}
uses entropy and the three specified graph/template inputs of
\cite{Montgomery}. Proposition~\ref{rsg:proposition} uses that theorem,
real alternating supports and the capacity lemmas; its only quantitative
rounding input is the edge-colouring estimate written in
Corollary~\ref{cor:rounding}. Independently, Lemmas~\ref{rt:lem:walks}
and~\ref{rou:power} supply real connectors. The compatible-cycle
theorem~\cite[Theorem 3.4]{BMMP} then supplies the comparator used by
Lemma~\ref{rt:thm:router}. These two branches meet at
Lemma~\ref{rou:ledger}. Lemma~\ref{rou:oneblock} proves the rooted
above-half Theorem~\ref{pf:theorem}, without invoking it. Theorem~
\ref{gaq:completion} covers the quotient case. Finally the real
decomposition and prefix in Section~\ref{gad:section} establish all
hypotheses used in the proof of Theorem~\ref{gen:theorem}.

Choose fixed density and gap bounds first, followed by the finite
gadget lengths. In the rooted construction (RP.4) has the uniform
constant $16/p_0$, so choosing $K$ before the errors is legitimate. The extension tolerance depends only on density and gap; therefore
the layer count and positive sampling rates can be chosen next.
Choose the candidate loss and fixed defect threshold sufficiently
small for these finitely many choices, and only then let the order
tend to infinity. In the decomposition argument the defects are $o(n)$ and consequently satisfy every resulting fixed threshold. No step infers an $o(1)$ candidate loss from a fixed positive defect.

For $L=\log(2m)$ the actual resource scales satisfy $m^{2/3}\log^4m=o(m^{4/5}),m^{4/5}=o(g),gL^{80}=o(b),b=o(\beta{}m)$, since $g=mL^{-12600}+O(1)$, $b=mL^{-12500}+O(1)$ and $\beta=L^{-12000}$. All module counts are fixed. Sublinear label repairs preserve the fixed positive Gram and reservoir margins;
the final matching defects are measured after those repairs.
Returned spare colours avoid the ordinary bank's exceptional palette
by its prior reservation, not by a probabilistic independence claim.

The three exact final checks are $(s-B_1-c)+(g+c)+b=s,(s-B_0-c)+(g+c)+b=s-1$, and $M+t\ell-1=D-1$. For a prefix of $a$ arcs with $Q$ vertices outside its final core, the suffix capacity is $m-D=|U|-d+Q$ and its concatenation has $a+(d-a-1)=d-1$ arcs. These equalities count real vertices and real colours. Completed
matching edges enter only counting estimates, and the single
assembly arc is deleted before the directed path is output.
