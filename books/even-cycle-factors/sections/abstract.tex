Let $H$ be an orientation of a cycle of even length $r\ge4$.
For every $\eta>0$, we prove that every sufficiently large digraph $G$ with $r\mid|G|$ and $\delta^0(G)\ge(1/2+\eta)|G|$ has an $H$-factor.
The coefficient $1/2$ is best possible, answering a question of Molla and Treglown.
Regularity and absorption reduce the proof to a perfect fractional tiling by homomorphic images of $H$, and height maps reduce most orientations to directed four-cycles and six-cycles.
For the two remaining families, we rule out deficient fractional covers using weight bounds in the neighbourhoods of a zero-weight vertex or a minimum-weight edge.
